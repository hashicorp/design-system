Raised the minimum supported Node.js version from 18 to 24. `ember-focus-trap` 2 requires Node.js 22, and 24 is the version HDS is built and tested on. Node.js 18 and 20 are end-of-life, and Node.js 22 reaches end-of-life in April 2027.

To migrate, use Node.js 24 or later to install and build your application.
