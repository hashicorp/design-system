## Border radius in Carbon

In Carbon, most border radiuses used in components are squared. Here are the differences in values compared to HDS:

![A table comparing the border radius values used in HDS and Carbon: extra small is 3px in HDS and 0px in Carbon, and is used in the Checkbox component; small is 5px in HDS and 0px in Carbon, and is used in the Badge, Button, and form components; medium is 6px in HDS and 0px in Carbon, and is used in the Card, RadioCard, Alert, and Toast components; large is 8px in HDS and 0px in Carbon, and is used in the Modal component.](/assets/foundations/borders/foundations-borders_carbonization.png)
