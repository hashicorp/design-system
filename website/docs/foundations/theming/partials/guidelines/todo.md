Theming allows your application to switch between different visual appearances at runtime, for example between a light and a dark theme.

Theming was introduced in version 7.0 of the design system, and is available only in the "carbonized" HDS. The "classic" HDS has a single, light-only visual language, and doesn't respond to theming.
