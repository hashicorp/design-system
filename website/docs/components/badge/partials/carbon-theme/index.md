## Component naming

HDS separates non-interactive metadata (Badge) from interactive metadata (Tag), whereas Carbon offers a single Tag component for both use cases.

There are several overlaps between the two systems:

![Comparison between Badges and Tags in hds, cds-0, and CDS White modes](/assets/components/badge/badge-tag.png)

## Unsupported in Carbon

Some HDS variants are not supported or available in Carbon. We have retained those variants in `cds` modes and aligned them to the closest Carbon foundations.

![Badge and Tag variants that are available in HDS but not in Carbon](/assets/components/badge/badge-tag-hds-only.png)
