# Helios writing guidelines

Any content written on the website should follow the formatting, stylistic, and organizational rules outlined in this document.

## Content principles

- Write for consumers - Write content to the expertise level of our consumers, who are well-versed in our field.
- Use plain language - Don't overly complicate content with buzzwords and jargon.
- Make it easy - Guide the reader through well-organized, scannable content. Do the hard work to make it simple.
- Use web standards - Prioritize the most important information first, with technical details later.

## Tone principles

- Confidence - We are the experts: Use a confident voice while staying kind.
- Trustworthy - We are trustworthy and authentic: Provide just enough transparency.
- Technical - Our consumers understand technical complexities: Provide technical details without overcomplicating things.
- Helpful - We provide guidance over control: Aim to educate and pair guidance with resources.

## Glossary

Use these terms consistently throughout our documentation.
- Application: What we call HashiCorp products. Use “application” instead of “website.”
- Consumers: Consumers are the users of the Helios Design System. They are often Frontend Engineers and Product Designers.
- Design Systems Team: Refers to the team responsible for maintaining the Helios Design System.
- End users: End users are the individuals who use HashiCorp products and services.
- Figma UI Kit(s): Figma UI Kit(s) are the published consumer-facing libraries in Figma.
- Helios Design System: The name of the design system. Spell out the first instance; subsequent usage can be HDS. Avoid overusing this; instead, consider “we” or “our”
- HDS: Shorthand for Helios Design System. Only to be used after the first spelled-out instance.
- Product team: Use “product team” when referring to an entire consuming team, instead of “application team.”
- The design system: Never use “a design system” as “a” is impersonal.
- Use: Never use “utilize.”
